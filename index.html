How to color

  1. Pick a section: Little Artists for simple pictures, or Mindful Coloring for detailed designs.
  2. Choose a category, then a picture.
  3. Pick a color from the palette at the bottom, or tap + for any color you like.
  4. Tap an area of the picture to fill it.
  5. Use + / − or pinch to zoom in on small parts. When zoomed in, drag with one finger to move around.

Frequently asked questions

How do I unlock the next picture?

Color every area of the current picture. When it's finished, the progress bar shows Next. Tap it to open the next picture. The first picture in each section is always unlocked.

I can't find the last spot to color.

Tap the 💡 left button next to the progress bar. Every area that still needs color will flash pink for a few seconds. Zoom in to fill very small areas.

Is my coloring saved?

Yes. Your progress saves automatically on your device as you color, so you can leave a picture and continue later.

How do I save a picture to my Photos?

Tap Save on the coloring screen. The first time, iOS asks for permission to add photos. If you chose "Don't Allow", open the Settings app → ColorBuddy → Photos and choose Add Photos Only.

How do I share a picture?

Tap Share on the coloring screen to send your picture through Messages, Mail or any app on your device.

Can I start a picture again?

Yes. Tap Reset on the coloring screen and confirm. Use Undo and Redo to step back and forward through your fills.

Does ColorBuddy need the internet?

No. ColorBuddy works fully offline. It has no ads, no accounts and no in-app purchases.

Will my progress move to a new device?

Coloring progress is stored only on the device you use. It isn't synced between devices, and it is removed if you delete the app.
