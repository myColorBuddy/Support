Privacy Policy

ColorBuddy: Calm Coloring

Support Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 14, 2026

The short version

ColorBuddy does not collect, store, share or sell any personal information. It has no accounts, no ads, no analytics, no tracking and no in-app purchases, and it does not connect to the internet. Everything you color stays on your device.

Who we are

ColorBuddy: Calm Coloring ("ColorBuddy", "the app") is made by ColorBuddy (mycolorbuddy.com) ("we", "us"). If you have any questions about this policy, contact us at support@mycolorbuddy.com.

Information we collect

None. We do not collect any personal information or usage data, and the app contains no third-party analytics, advertising or tracking tools.

Information stored on your device

To save your coloring, the app stores the following only on your device:

  • Your colored pictures and small preview images
  • Which pictures you have finished, and when each was last changed

This information is never sent to us or anyone else. It is removed when you reset a picture or delete the app. If your device's own backups (such as iCloud Backup) are turned on, it may be included in those backups, which are handled by Apple under Apple's privacy policy.

Photos permission

If you tap Save, iOS asks for permission to add photos. ColorBuddy uses this only to save your colored picture to your photo library. The app cannot see, read or upload any of your existing photos. You can change this at any time in Settings → ColorBuddy → Photos.

Sharing

If you tap Share, your picture is passed to the iOS share sheet, and you choose where it goes (for example Messages or Mail). ColorBuddy does not see or keep a record of what you share. Any app you share with handles your picture under its own privacy policy.

Children's privacy

ColorBuddy is designed for families, including young children. Because the app collects no personal information from anyone, it collects none from children. There are no ads, no links to websites inside the coloring experience, no chat and no purchases. We comply with the U.S. Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) and similar laws, including the EU and UK GDPR, by not collecting personal data at all.

Changes to this policy

If we change how ColorBuddy handles information (for example, if a future version adds new features), we will update this page and the effective date above before the change takes effect.

Contact

Questions or concerns about privacy? Email support@mycolorbuddy.com.
